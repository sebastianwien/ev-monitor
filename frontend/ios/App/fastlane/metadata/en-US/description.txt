EV Monitor is your digital charging log for your electric car. Add your charging sessions - or import them automatically - and see at a glance what your car really consumes, what charging costs you and how far you actually get in everyday driving.

Real data instead of WLTP brochure figures: kWh per 100 km, cost per charge and realistic range - from your own trips.

YOUR FEATURES
• Charging log: every session cleanly documented, AC and DC
• Costs under control: charging cost per month, per 100 km and per session
• Real consumption: kWh/100 km from real data instead of manufacturer claims
• Real range: based on your actual driving profile
• Comprehensive dashboard: trends, seasonal patterns and rich insights
• Community comparison: how does your car do against the same model?
• Automatic import: incl. SpritMonitor, Tronity, Tessie, Go-E Wallbox

TRANSPARENT AND SECURE
• Open source - the code is publicly auditable
• GDPR-compliant, hosted in Germany
• Privacy-first: no exact GPS coordinates, only anonymized geohashes
• Your data is yours - export or delete it anytime

Whether you drive a lot or charge occasionally: EV Monitor turns your charging data into real knowledge about your EV.
